Kiln & firings

Plan the load before anyone opens the lid.

Members drop work on the greenware or glaze shelf and log it in a few taps. You see what is waiting, how much room it needs and which firing it belongs to, then build the load on a shelf-by-shelf plan instead of in your head.

Member shelves

Every piece has an address.

Members can see where their work is, from greenware to bisque to the pickup shelf, without asking you. Pieces that sit too long get a polite nudge, and you get your shelves back.

Shelf labels you can print. Each shelf gets a short code. Members log a piece to a code; you move it when it fires. That is the whole system.
