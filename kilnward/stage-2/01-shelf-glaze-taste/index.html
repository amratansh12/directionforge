A week, the way it really runs

Each day is a shelf. Classes, open studio and kiln work rest on it in the order they happen, so you can see at a glance that Tuesday’s wheel class is full and Sunday is quiet.

Members book themselves. They pick a class or an open-studio slot from their phone, and the count on the shelf changes. No more matching names in a spreadsheet.

Full classes keep a waiting list. When someone cancels, the next person is offered the place, and you hear about it after it is settled.

Kiln work sits in the same week. Loading and unloading are booked like anything else, so nobody plans a glaze night on the evening the kiln is being packed.

Know whose bowl is whose

Every member has a shelf in Kilnward, the same as in the studio. Each piece carries its stage, from drying greenware to ready to collect, and moves along when a firing is unloaded. Members see their own shelf; you see all of them.
