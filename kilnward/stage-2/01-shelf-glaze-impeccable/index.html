Plan the load before you lift a shelf.

Put each piece on a kiln shelf by its height, check the clearance, and keep the firing schedule beside the load it belongs to. When the kiln is unloaded, the people whose pots were inside hear about it.

What it takes off your plate.

The booking spreadsheet
Members book open-studio benches and class places themselves. Full classes keep a waitlist, and a cancelled place goes to the next person on it.
The group chat
When a firing is unloaded, everyone with work inside gets a note saying which pieces came out and which shelf they’re on. Nobody has to ask.
The whiteboard by the kiln
Loads, schedules and cones live with each firing, so whoever is on kiln duty this week can see what goes in, in what order, and when it can be opened.
