A week on the board

What comes off the wall

The spreadsheet
Bookings, class lists and the waiting list, with places that free up when someone cancels.
The group chat
Reminders the evening before, and the “kiln’s on, keep the door shut” notice, sent once.
The whiteboard by the kiln
Load plans, cone numbers and who is unloading on Saturday.

Plan every load, then fire

Members queue their pieces with a size and a firing. You fill the shelves on screen before anyone lifts the lid, and bisque ware in the glaze queue gets caught here, not on Saturday morning.

Every shelf has a name on it
