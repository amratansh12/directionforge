The whole week waits on the kiln.

Kilnward plans your studio around the firing: who is booked on which wheel, what goes on which kiln shelf, when the door opens again, and whose bowls are waiting by the sink.

Start free trial Read this week’s contents

Monday the beginners’ wheel class fills up by lunchtime. Tuesday somebody asks, in the group chat, whether her mugs made the bisque. Wednesday the kiln is half full and the shelf by the door is overflowing. By Thursday the whiteboard says three different things, and only one of them is right.

None of this is unusual. A small studio runs on a firing rhythm that calendars were never built for: classes feed the shelves, the shelves feed the kiln, the kiln decides when anyone can take their work home. Kilnward keeps those four things in one book, so the answer to “is it out yet?” is already written down.

This issue follows one firing week through a studio with two kilns and ninety-odd members. Every booking, load and shelf in it is sample data, set the way you would see it in the app.

MondayKilnward · Issue 07p. 2

A week of bookings, kept in one book.

Classes and open-studio sessions sit on the same page, because they share the same wheels, the same tables and, eventually, the same kiln. Set a session’s places once; members book themselves in, and the count on the left is the count you trust.

Firings appear in the week as well. When Kiln B’s load closes on Wednesday afternoon, the people booked for Wednesday morning can see it, and so can you.

Nothing here asks you to change how the studio runs. It writes down the week you already have.

WednesdayKilnward · Issue 07p. 3

Loading the kiln without a clipboard.

Work comes in all week: a class’s worth of bowls on Monday, three planters on Tuesday, a platter that will need a shelf to itself. Kilnward lets you plan the load as it arrives, shelf by shelf, with the member’s name on every piece.

By the time the load closes you already know what is going in, what is waiting for the next firing, and who to tell.

ThursdayKilnward · Issue 07p. 4

The firing schedule, on the wall and in every pocket.

Bisque and glaze, both kilns, one schedule. Each firing carries its cone, when the load closes, when it starts and when the door can open. When a firing is done, everyone with work inside gets a note.

SaturdayKilnward · Issue 07p. 5

Whose pots are on which shelf.

Every member has a shelf, and every piece on it is somewhere between wet clay and done. Kilnward keeps that list, so a returning member can find their work and you can see which shelves have been full for a fortnight.

Pieces move along by themselves as firings finish: out of Kiln B on Saturday, back onto the member’s shelf, marked ready for glaze.
