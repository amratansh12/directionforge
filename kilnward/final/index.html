FRI 1606:00

Nobody lifts the lid early.

After the elements go off, Kilnward estimates when the lid can be cracked and when the kiln is safe to open, and keeps the kiln room shut on the booking sheet until the unload. The rest of the studio carries on around it.

SAT 1709:00

Out of the kiln, onto eleven shelves.

The unload follows the load plan in reverse. Each member’s work goes to a collection shelf, and the eleven people with pieces in K2 get one message each with their shelf. Nobody else is pinged.
