FRI 1606:00

Nobody lifts the lid before 14:20.

After the elements go off, Kilnward estimates when the kiln can be cracked and when it is safe to open, and keeps the kiln room closed on the booking sheet until then. The rest of the studio carries on around it.

FRI 1615:00

Out of the kiln, onto eleven shelves.

The unload follows the load plan in reverse. Each member's work goes to a collection shelf, and the eleven people with pieces in K2 get one message each with their shelf. Nobody else is pinged.
